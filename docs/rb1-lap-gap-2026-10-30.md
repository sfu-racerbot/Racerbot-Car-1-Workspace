# Car 1's lap gap on 2026-10-30

> **Who this is for:** anyone driving car 1 (`rb1`) after 2026-11-02, and anyone comparing the two cars' lap times.
> **Read first:** [Course analysis](course-analysis.md) for how runs are located on a map.
> **You'll be able to:** say why car 1 lapped slower than car 2 on 2026-10-30, reproduce every number here, and run car 1 safely for the first time after its motor command gain changed.
> **Time:** 10 minutes to read; about 15 minutes to reproduce.

## Highlights

- **Car 1 delivered 0.79 of every speed it commanded.** Its motor command gain was 3650 eRPM per m/s, and at racing speed the car needs about 4600 eRPM per true m/s. Car 2 needs the same 4600 and commands with 4614, so it delivers 1.01 of its command. `/odom` hid this on car 1 because it reads the same wheels: it over-read true speed by 15 to 17%.
- **That costs car 1 about 4.4 s a lap at equal settings.** In the simulator, on the measured 2026-10-30 course and floor, car 1's gap_follow laps a standing-start lap in 24.1 s as raced and 19.7 s with the corrected gain (median of 6 seeds each, no contact). Car 1's real standing-start lap that day was 24.1 s.
- **The recorded laps do not show 2 s like-for-like, because the cars ran different speed caps.** Car 2's steady 20.6 to 21.0 s laps came from map_course at a 3.5 m/s cap; car 1 ran a 5.0 m/s cap, which its gain turned into about 3.9 m/s. On flying laps measured sector by sector, car 1 was 0.44 s behind those laps.
- **The other leads explain none of it.** Compute, LiDAR, battery and code were equal or favoured car 1, and steering damping (on for car 1 only that day) made it faster, not slower.
- **Fix:** car 1's command gain is now 4600 (`src/racerbot_car/cars/rb1/calibration/vesc.yaml`). **Every commanded speed on car 1 is about 26% faster in reality**, manual teleop included, so its first run follows [the procedure below](#first-run-after-the-gain-change).

## Cause: the motor command gain

`ackermann_to_vesc_node` turns a `/drive` speed into a motor command of `speed_to_erpm_gain × speed` eRPM, and the VESC holds that eRPM. If the gain is lower than the eRPM the car really needs per m/s, every command comes out slow, and `/odom` (the same eRPM divided by the odometry gain) cannot see it.

`tools/run_analysis/speed_gain.py` measures it over each LB hold, against the map:

| Car, holds | Map distance | Map ÷ commanded distance | Gain that delivers the command | `/odom` ÷ map |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1, solo race runs `180531` + `190734` | 232 m | **0.793** | 3650 / 0.793 = **4604** | 1.15 to 1.16 |
| 1, all ten holds of the day | 556 m | 0.815 | 4481 | 1.05 to 1.16 |
| 2, five holds (`181003`, `183134`, map_course ×3) | 752 m | **1.013** | 4556 (runs at 4614) | 1.02 to 1.04 |

The simulator's own floor fit for car 2 (`tools/f1tenth_sim/sim_fidelity/calibration.py`, `speed_command_scale = 1.004`, `odom_speed_scale = 1.033`) was made independently and agrees with car 2's row, which checks the method.

The eRPM each car needs per true m/s rises with speed, because the tyres slip more under power: on straights both cars need about 4000 below 1.5 m/s and 4450 to 4700 between 2 and 4 m/s. Car 1's odometry gain of 4040 came from slow 2 to 2.6 m tape runs on 2026-10-21, so it holds for slow driving only.

The motor was not the limit. Both VESCs reached their commanded eRPM (measured over commanded 0.98 to 1.04).

**What it did to car 1's driving.** gap_follow's caps are all in commanded speed. At 0.79 realisation, car 1 drove its curve cap at 0.79² = 0.62 of the lateral acceleration the cap allows, and its 5 m/s² acceleration ramp gave about 3.9 m/s² on the floor. `intent_report.py` shows the acceleration ceiling holding car 1 down for 34% of ticks in `180531`, against 17% for car 2 in `185036`.

<a id="steering-limit"></a>**Steering limit.** With the corrected gain, car 1's simulated gap_follow at car 2's 0.349 rad limit (and `arc_guard_max_correction` 0.1745) laps in 19.2 s against 19.7 s at 0.33 rad, with no contact in 6 seeds. The floor model's steering linkage is car 2's, so this sizes the limit, not car 1's real linkage. Car 1's full stick of 0.38 rad reaches both sides, so 0.349 fits its servo, but raising an autonomy limit is a team decision and is not part of this change.

## The fix

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `src/racerbot_car/cars/rb1/calibration/vesc.yaml` | `/**` `speed_to_erpm_gain` 3650 → **4600**; odometry gain stays −4040 |
| `docs/hardware-reference.md`, `docs/car1-vs-car2.md`, `docs/git-setup.md` | the new value and its measurement |
| `tools/neural_racer/neural_racer_train/gpu_sim/params.py` | car 1's top commanded speed 23250 / 4600 = 5.05 m/s (was 6.37) |
| `tools/f1tenth_sim` | nothing to change: `calibration_for_car` reads the overlay, so car 1's simulated top speed is now 5.05 m/s |
| `src/racerbot_launch/test/test_speed_limits_fit_vesc.py` (new) | every shipped top speed × each car's command gain stays inside `speed_max` (23250); 5.0 × 4600 = 23000 |
| `src/racerbot_launch/test/test_car_profiles.py` | the overlay must equal the documented gain; the frozen car 1 oracle (`ae7ceeb`) keeps every other key exact |

**What changes on the floor.** Every speed command on car 1 is about 26% faster in reality (4600 / 3650). At racing speed it now gets what it asks for, as car 2 does. Below 1.5 m/s it gets about 15% more than it asks for, as car 2 does. Manual teleop's full stick (5.0 m/s commanded) goes from about 3.9 to about 5.0 m/s real. The true top speed does not change: the VESC's `speed_max` of 23250 eRPM is still about 5.0 m/s real, now reached at a 5.05 m/s command. The margin car 1 had been running below its own caps is gone, which is why the first run is staged.

**Not changed: the odometry gain.** At −4040 `/odom` keeps reading 15 to 17% above true speed at racing speed, and about right below 1.5 m/s. That makes TTC and gap_follow's braking ceiling act early, which is the safe side. Changing it to car 1's racing-speed value (about −4600) would make them act later, and slam_toolbox and the gyro odometry use it too, so it needs its own decision and its own run.

## First run after the gain change

**At the car, LB in hand, spotter present.**

1. **Update and check the value.** Pull the branch, then build:

   ```bash
   cd ~/racerbot-ws && git pull
   colcon build --symlink-install --packages-select racerbot_car racerbot_launch gap_follow
   source install/setup.bash
   ros2 launch f1tenth_stack bringup_launch.py
   ```

   In a second terminal:

   ```bash
   ros2 param get /ackermann_to_vesc_node speed_to_erpm_gain     # 4600.0
   ros2 param get /vesc_to_odom_node speed_to_erpm_gain          # -4040.0, unchanged
   ```

   Stop if either differs.

2. **Wheels off the ground.** Put the car on its stand, wheels clear. Start `ros2 launch f1tenth_stack teleop_launch.py`, and in another terminal `ros2 topic echo /commands/motor/speed`.
   - Hold LB, push the speed stick forward a little: the wheels turn forward, and the echoed value is 4600 × the commanded speed (stick fraction × 5.0 m/s). A quarter stick is about 5750.
   - Full stick forward for one second: about 23000, never above 23250.
   - Release LB: the command drops to 0 and the wheels stop.
3. **Floor, low speed.** Open space, nothing within 3 m. Run gap_follow capped low:

   ```bash
   ros2 launch gap_follow gap_follow_launch.py max_speed:=1.0
   ```

   Hold LB for a short stretch, then release. At this cap the car now moves at about 1.1 m/s, where it used to move at about 0.9. Then drive teleop gently: the stick is more sensitive than before.
4. **Course, normal settings.** Drive gap_follow on the course as usual. `auto_record` records it.

## Confirming the fix from that run

On the dev box, after the run is copied to `data/rb1/racerbot_runs/`. Use a map of the same day's course, such as the one `map_course` saved:

```bash
rb python3 tools/run_analysis/loc.py data/rb1/racerbot_runs/<run> --map <map dir>
rb python3 tools/run_analysis/speed_gain.py data/rb1/racerbot_runs/<run>
```

**Working when:** the run fits the map at 0.9 or better, and the pooled realisation is 0.97 to 1.05 with a suggested gain of 4400 to 4750. A realisation outside that range means the floor or tyres differ from 2026-10-30; set the gain to the suggested value from a run with at least 150 m of LB-held driving at race pace, and repeat the staged first run.

**map_course after the change.** `mapping_max_speed` (3.5 m/s) now runs at about 3.5 m/s real on car 1, where it used to run at about 2.8. The car 1 SLAM replay figure of about 3.3 m/s in [car1-vs-car2.md](car1-vs-car2.md#imu-feature-thresholds) was made at the old gain. Check its `slam_report` after the first mapping run, or map with `max_speed:=2.8` until then.

## Decisions left open

- **Odometry gain** (above): keep −4040, or move to the racing-speed value. It changes TTC, the braking ceiling and SLAM input on car 1.
- **Car 1's autonomy steering limit:** 0.33 rad, against car 2's 0.349. The simulator puts it at about 0.5 s a lap ([above](#steering-limit)); raising it needs wheels-off and floor checks of its own.
- **Three edits to existing tests, called out for review:**
  - `test_car_profiles.py` gains a `RECALIBRATED` table. It relaxes nothing: the frozen car 1 oracle must still read 3650 at `ae7ceeb`, and the overlay must read exactly 4600.
  - `tools/neural_racer/tests/test_dynamics_parity.py`: the oracle is still `vesc.yaml`'s `speed_max` over car 1's command gain, now 23250 / 4600.
  - `src/racerbot_car/test/test_no_direct_calibration_reads.py` allow-lists `speed_gain.py`, which reads a recorded run's parameter snapshot on purpose: a run from before a recalibration was driven with the old gain.

## Limitations

- The simulator's floor and tyre model is car 2's, measured on 2026-10-30. Car 1's own grip may differ, which shifts the simulated seconds, not the realisation.
- Car 1 had two solo race-pace runs (232 m). The suggested gain over all ten holds of the day, including slower and two-car holds, is 4481, so 4600 is the race-pace end of the range.
- Real per-sector times rest on 2 to 5 passes per sector for car 1.

## Reproduce

Every number above comes from one script, which writes only under `~/.cache/racerbot/`:

```bash
RB=rb bash tools/run_analysis/studies/rb1_lap_gap_2026_10_30/run_all.sh
```

**Working when:** it prints ten numbered sections ending in the simulator's per-sector table. On a car, source ROS and drop `RB=rb`. The simulator steps need `tools/f1tenth_sim/setup.sh` run once. The scripts are in [`tools/run_analysis/studies/rb1_lap_gap_2026_10_30/`](../tools/run_analysis/studies/rb1_lap_gap_2026_10_30/).
